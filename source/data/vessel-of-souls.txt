ENTRY|Overview
T|ÿcOVessel of SoulsÿcN is a special mythical unique amulet that can consume 5 organs at a time to get various bonuses based on the organs consumed.
T|The vessel spawns with 2000 souls, and every recipe consumes a number of souls. There are four tiers of stats, and each tier has specific properties.
T|You need to complete each tier in order before you can use the next one (for example, tier 3 recipes cannot be used until tiers 1 and 2 are complete).
T|Every tier has a capacity of 5 recipes, except tier 4 (the last one), which is a bit more special.
T|You will be notified when each tier is exhausted with special orange text on the item.
T|Once you exhaust all tiers, you can transform the ÿcOVessel of SoulsÿcN into one of many possible mythical weapon bases.
T|ÿcOVessel of SoulsÿcN is also the only equippable unique item in the game that can be anointed.
T|ÿcOVessel of SoulsÿcN cannot be corrupted while it has unconsumed souls.
T|ÿcOVessel of SoulsÿcN cannot be worn as an amulet. You need to complete its crafting process and transform it into a weapon first.
